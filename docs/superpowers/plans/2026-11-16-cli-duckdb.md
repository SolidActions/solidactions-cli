## Review Focus

2. **A query on a `duckdb_file` database with nothing published, or while the kind's flag is off** (Task 3): the app answers `no_published_version` or `feature_disabled` with a `{code, message}` error. Expected: the CLI prints the app's own sentence and exits 1, never "The database response was invalid." (today's behaviour on any success shape, `src/commands/database.ts` line 1616) and never a stack.
